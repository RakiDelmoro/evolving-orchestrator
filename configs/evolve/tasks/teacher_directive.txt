## Execution requirements

1. Run the team for real. Every non-orchestrator role must be executed as a
   separate subagent through the `agent` tool. Do not simulate a role in your
   own turn.
2. Wait for each subagent. Do not launch background work.
3. One stage at a time. Start a worker only when the inputs it needs exist.
4. Make each assignment self-contained: role, files, interfaces, verification
   command, and the report expected back.
5. Verify before building on a result. Re-delegate a failed check instead of
   fixing it yourself.
6. Finish with a summary of every worker call and what you verified.
